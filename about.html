About Breakfast, Brunch & Dinner

Charleston, South Carolina

Breakfast, Brunch & Dinner is an independent recipe site based in Charleston, South Carolina. We publish practical recipes for the three meals most people cook at home, along with cooking technique and notes on eating out casually in the city we live in. The goal is a small collection of recipes that work every time, not a large archive of ones that might.

How the recipes are written

Every recipe is cooked repeatedly in a home kitchen before it is published. We use standard grocery store ingredients, common pans, and a regular home range. If a step can be removed without hurting the result, it is removed. If an ingredient is only there for show, it is left out. Each recipe lists realistic timing, including the prep, and tells you what to look for when a timer is not reliable. Recipes are updated when we find a better method, and the changes are made in place so there is only ever one current version.

What the site covers

The recipe collection is organized by meal. Breakfast recipes are quick and built around eggs. Brunch recipes are meant for a table of people on a slow morning. Dinner recipes are one-pan meals for weeknights. The cooking tips page collects the habits that apply across all of them. The lifestyle page is about casual dining in Charleston from a local point of view: when to go, where to sit, and how to eat well downtown without a reservation.

Our partner, Sweet Palm Coffee

Breakfast, Brunch & Dinner partners with Sweet Palm Coffee, a cafe at 471 King Street in downtown Charleston. Sweet Palm Coffee & Food serves coffee and a full food menu, including the chicken caesar wrap and the breakfast burrito that inspired two of the recipes on this site. We recommend the cafe because we eat there, and because a recipe site should be able to point to a real counter where the dish is done well. When a recipe on this site is modeled on a Sweet Palm menu item, the recipe says so.

Editorial standards

Recipes and articles on this site are original. Partner mentions are identified as partner mentions. We do not publish health or nutrition claims, and cooking temperatures follow standard food safety guidance. If you cook something from the site and it does not turn out, the method is what we want to fix, so the recipe pages are revised whenever a clearer instruction is found.
