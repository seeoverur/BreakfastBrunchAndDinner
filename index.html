Welcome

Recipes for Every Meal of the Day

Home cooking from Charleston, South Carolina

Breakfast, Brunch & Dinner is a recipe site built around the three meals people actually cook at home. Every recipe here uses a short ingredient list, one or two pans, and steps you can follow on a weeknight without reading them twice. We cook out of a small Charleston kitchen, so the food leans Southern and coastal: grits, shrimp, eggs, biscuits, and a lot of lemon.

The site is split the way a day is split. Morning recipes are fast and built on eggs. Brunch recipes take a little longer and feed a table. Dinner recipes are one-pan meals that leave very few dishes behind. Alongside the recipes you will find a page of cooking tips that apply to almost anything you make, and a lifestyle page about eating out casually in Charleston the way people who live here do it.

Soft-Scrambled Breakfast Burrito

Posted on November 9, 2026

A good breakfast burrito comes down to two things: eggs that are still soft when they go into the tortilla, and potatoes that are crisp enough to hold their shape. Most homemade versions fail because the eggs are cooked dry and then steamed again inside the wrap. This one pulls the eggs off the heat early, layers the cheese against the warm tortilla so it melts on contact, and finishes the whole burrito seam-side down in the pan so it stays closed. It takes about twenty-five minutes and it freezes well, which makes it one of the most useful recipes on the site.

Lowcountry Shrimp and Grits

Posted on November 4, 2026

Shrimp and grits is the dish Charleston is known for, and it is far easier than its reputation suggests. Stone-ground grits need time and stirring, not skill. The shrimp cook in the same pan as the bacon in under four minutes. A squeeze of lemon and a few dashes of hot sauce pull the pan drippings into a quick sauce. Our version skips the heavy cream and the flour-thickened gravy, so the shrimp stay the point of the plate. Make it for brunch with a table of people, or halve it for a quiet dinner.

Homemade Chicken Caesar Wrap

Posted on October 28, 2026

The chicken caesar wrap is a lunch counter classic that turns into an easy dinner at home. The trick is dressing the romaine lightly, slicing the chicken thin, and adding something crunchy so the wrap has texture all the way through. Our dressing is a five-minute shortcut made with mayonnaise, lemon, Dijon, garlic, and parmesan, with no raw egg and no blender. Warm the tortilla before you roll it and it will fold without tearing.

Partner Site

Sweet Palm Coffee on King Street

471 King Street, Charleston, SC

Sweet Palm Coffee is our partner site and the cafe we point people to when they would rather order breakfast than cook it. Sweet Palm Coffee & Food is a locally run cafe at 471 King Street in downtown Charleston, serving coffee alongside a full food menu from a walk-up counter in the middle of the Upper King shopping and dining district.

Two items on the menu line up with recipes on this site. The Sweet Palm chicken caesar wrap is the grab-and-go lunch that inspired our homemade version, and the breakfast burrito is the reason we spent a month working out how to keep scrambled eggs soft inside a tortilla. If you are in Charleston, try the originals first and then cook ours at home to compare. The cafe also serves dirty sodas and ice cream, which makes it a useful stop at any hour of a King Street afternoon.

Current hours and the full menu are on the Sweet Palm Coffee website.
